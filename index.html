Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have compiled answers to the most common queries

How does a MyLux NFC card work?

Tap the card against a compatible phone or scan its QR code. Your digital profile opens instantly in the recipient’s browser—no app required.

Can I update my details after my card arrives?

Yes. Update your contact details, links, social profiles and profile content without replacing the physical card.

What happens if NFC is not available?

Every MyLux card includes a QR backup. Anyone with a camera-enabled phone can scan it to open the same digital profile.

Can I customise the card design?

Yes. Choose the material, colour, finish, name, designation, logo placement and QR-code placement before placing your order.

Is my information kept private?

You control the information your profile displays. Private phone numbers can remain hidden, and MyLux Find supports secure finder-to-owner messaging.
